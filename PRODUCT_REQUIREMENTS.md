# Product Requirements

The active product contract is `BUILD_ROADMAP.md` plus `LEAN_PRODUCT_SCOPE.md`, subject to explicit user decisions. `AI_Editorial_Board_Spec.md` remains authoritative for requirements not superseded by the accepted lean direction. This document intentionally does not duplicate either source.

The MVP is a local, single-user editorial workspace that begins with conversational idea intake, preserves human approval, uses configurable providers behind a model-independent interface, and records cost, review, publication, feedback, and retrospective history.
